How it calculates

Simple enough to explain on stage

1

Bill to units

Monthly bill is divided by the selected city's electricity tariff.

2

Units to system size

Required kW is estimated from city solar generation and 30 monthly days.

3

Roof check

The tool limits capacity using about 90 sq ft per kW of usable rooftop space.

4

Cost and impact

It applies subsidy slabs, estimates savings, payback, and yearly CO2 avoided.

Open source

Built for reuse by students, NGOs, and housing societies.

The app runs fully in the browser, costs nothing to host on GitHub Pages, and can be improved by updating the city data and formula assumptions in one JavaScript file.
