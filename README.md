# ☀️ SOLAREX

### Smart Rooftop Solar Assessment & Energy Savings Calculator

**Solarex** is a web-based solar energy assessment tool that helps users understand the potential of installing a rooftop solar system based on their **location, electricity consumption, and available roof area**.

The platform converts basic user inputs into practical solar insights such as the **estimated number of panels, expected generation, installation cost, monthly savings, payback period, and environmental impact**.

Solarex is designed to make solar planning simpler, faster, and easier to understand without requiring users to have technical knowledge of solar power systems.

---

## 🚀 Features

### ☀️ Solar System Estimation
Estimate the approximate solar capacity required based on the user's electricity consumption and available rooftop area.

### 📍 Location-Based Analysis
Use the selected location to improve solar potential and generation estimates.

### ⚡ Energy Generation
Estimate how much electricity the proposed solar installation can generate.

### 💰 Cost Estimation
Calculate an approximate installation cost based on the required solar capacity.

### 📉 Monthly Savings
Estimate potential savings from generating electricity through rooftop solar.

### ⏱️ Payback Period
Calculate approximately how long it may take for the solar investment to recover through electricity savings.

### 🌱 Environmental Impact
Estimate the amount of **CO₂ emissions avoided** by using renewable solar energy.

## 🎯 Objective

The main goal of Solarex is to answer a simple question:

> **"Is rooftop solar suitable for my location and energy usage, and how much can I potentially save?"**

Instead of presenting users with complex solar calculations, Solarex converts them into understandable and actionable information.
